# BROMAX's Exofall 1.0.0 (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge)

First release.

**Requires:** GeckoLib 4.7+, Curios 9+. **Optional:** Patchouli (the Exofall Field Guide), JEI (5x5 recipe pages).

## Meteors
- Four kinds of huge meteor land half-buried in craters across the Overworld: **Swamp, Hive, Desert and Rainforest**.
- Each is sealed in blast-proof Shellrock that only the **Crustbreaker** can mine.
- Each has its own interior biome, alien woods (Stiltreed, Combwood, Ribwood, Veilpine) and plants that only grow on Glowmarl.

## Creatures
- 33 giant bugs that hunt each other and you. Every ambusher has a tell that gives it away.

## The Warrens
- Two-level dungeons under every meteor: mineshaft tunnels, rooms, spiral stairs and a vault.
- Entrance and nest huts on the surface.
- Four nest spawners that keep letting the meteor's bugs out.

## Bosses
- A sealed dome at every meteor's heart, opened with that meteor's sigil.
- Four bosses with telegraphed attacks and an intro cinematic: **Terror of the Bog, Waxen Queen, Dozen-legged Wrath and Apex Mantis**.

## Gear
- Shellrock Crafting Table with a 5x5 grid. It crafts normal recipes too.
- 4 armour sets with perks: **Mireweave, Crystallized Honey, Reinforced Carapace and Ancientstone**.
- 4 legendary weapons with on-hit effects: **Glasswing Scythe, Royal Grandhammer, Quadblade of the Sands and Serrated Longsword**.
- 6 baubles (Curios).
- Everything is crafted from Warren-bug drops and boss drops.

## Pets
- Tame eight of the bugs with treats. They become passive and never despawn.
- Breed two tamed adults of the same kind for a tamed baby.
- Cross-breeding into hybrids comes in a later version.

## Also
- **Exofall Field Guide** (Patchouli): every mob, mechanic and recipe. Craft it from a book and flint.
- 17 advancements.
- Config: `gear.damageMultiplier` and `gear.armorMultiplier` scale Exofall weapon damage and armour/toughness for other packs.
